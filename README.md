# Module 3 Skills Notes

## **Posting 1: Health Data Analyst 1**

Required skills: 
- strong communication skills
- ability to collaborate and work with other team members
- masters degree in a quantitative or health field OR a bachelors degree and 2 years of relevant experience
- exprience with R, SAS, SPSS, Python
- organzing and managing data sets


Preferred skills: 
- PhD student
- experience working with public health and survey data
- experience conducting quantitative analyses and interpreting results


## **Posting 2: Senior Revenue Cycle Analyst**

Required skills: 
- system analysis and troubleshooting
- experience designing automated controls 
- strong critical thinking and problem solving skills 
- experience analyzing and managing large data sets
- EPIC certification 
- Masters degree and 4 years of experiernce OR a bachelors degree and 6 years of relevant experience


Preferred skills: 
- familiarity with ICD-10 and other hospital billing systems 
- knowledge of patient care process
- knowledge and experience with EPIC 


## **Skills appearing in both postings:**
- communication
- critical thinking
- technology skills (coding, data sets)
- collaboration/teamwork

### **Notes:**

Although both jobs were fairly different (one a more entry level position, the other a senior position), there were still many similarities between the postings. The specific coding and data skills varied, but the general NACE competency skills were a commonality in both jobs. Communication, critical thinking, technology, and teamwork skills are both hinted at many times in both postings. These more general skills are not often listed outright in the job posting, but hidden between the lines of the skills required and preferred.
