# Lucy Ballanti

Hi, and welcome! I am a sophomore at Oregon State University studying data science and medical humanities. 

Before studying at OSU, I completed the health sciences track at my high school and studied anatomy and medical assisting. A congenital heart defect that required two surgeries meant that I spent a not insignificant amount of time in hospitals and doctors offices, and in a strange sort of way, it is what felt most familiar and comforting to me. It took a while for me to realize that I did not want to work as a clinician, but instead work to improve healthcare and patient outcomes on a larger, population level. 

Studying data science combines my love for statistics with coding and analysis to tell a larger story. The stories I hope to tell are ones that shape and inform policy to improve lives, and ones that make strides in clinical research that could save patients. 

I currently work for the School of Nutrition and Public Health at OSU, helping to track the spread of Foodborne diseases in Oregon. When not in school or working, my interests include reading, painting, and playing guitar.
